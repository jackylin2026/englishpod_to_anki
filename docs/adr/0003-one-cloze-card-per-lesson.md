# One cloze card per lesson, with blanks from Key Vocabulary alone

Each lesson produces exactly one Anki note: the full dialogue with its Key Vocabulary terms blanked out, and the glossary, IPA and dialogue audio on the answer side.

Anki convention, and the tool's first recommendation, was one card per vocabulary term — atomic cards the scheduler can space individually. That was rejected in favour of the design the user had already built by hand and studied from for lesson 108. The per-term alternative remains available later as an additional note type, which would leave these lesson cards untouched.

The blanks are all of Key Vocabulary, not a selection. The hand-made card blanks only the words its author had not already learned — seven of the thirteen its lesson supports — so the sample card is a reference for *shape*, and must not be used as a fixture to reproduce exactly. The tool blanks every term the dialogue carries; what is worth studying is decided by review, not by generation.

The cost is deliberate and worth recording, because it will look like a mistake to anyone who has not seen it: one card carries around fifteen glossary entries and ninety seconds of audio, and Anki's scheduler cannot tell a failure on one blank from a failure on another. If these cards prove too heavy in review, that is the pressure to revisit this decision — not a bug.
